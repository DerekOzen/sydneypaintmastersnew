Frequently asked questions

What does a registered NDIS provider actually do? +
A registered provider delivers supports under the NDIS quality and safeguards framework and can be verified publicly. The day-to-day job is practical: match the right worker, set routines that work in your home, and keep supports aligned to your plan goals.
Do you support both Melbourne and Sydney? +
Yes. We have a local team in each city. Coverage depends on your suburb and the times you need. Tell us your suburb and preferred windows and we will confirm whether there is a match for consistent, ongoing support.
Will you arrange an interpreter? +
Yes, and we pay for it. The interpreter is on the call before anything is signed, so the agreement and plan documents are understood, not just the spoken conversation.
Do I have to sign at the first meeting? +
No. Nobody signs on the first visit. We read the agreement with you, leave a copy, and give you time to decide at home.
Do you offer in-home care and community participation? +
Yes. Our core supports are Assist Personal Activities, Household Tasks, Community Participation, and Nursing Care where it is funded and appropriate. We keep scope honest rather than claiming everything.

Talk to the team in your city

Tell us your suburb, the supports you need most, and the times that matter to your household. We will confirm the next step and how worker matching works.
